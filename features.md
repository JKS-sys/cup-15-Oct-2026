# Cup — features

Ready to paste into the ipconfig website. Updated with every round of work.
Last updated: 12-Nov-2026 · after Cup 1.0.3

## Free (everyone)

Cup has no paid plan: every feature below is free for personal use.

### Store
- Discover page with featured apps and tools, categories and search across the whole Pour catalogue
- Apps and Tools pages with several layouts, and a details sheet for each package (version, home page, what it needs, what needs it)
- One-click install, update, reinstall and remove — all through Pour, never Homebrew
- Install command shown for every package, with a working copy button
- Command palette (Ctrl/Cmd + K) to find any app, tool or command

### Your computer
- Installed page with the size on disk of every app and tool, and totals
- Drag across the checkboxes to select or deselect many rows at once
- Updates page: what is out of date, update one or all
- Brewfile snapshots: save, restore, export
- Taps page, with a clear warning before a default Pour tap is removed
- Care page: doctor, cleanup, index update, move from Homebrew

### Log
- Every Pour command with its full output, in its own log box
- Summary tiles: runs, done, running, failed, lines, warnings
- A drawn mark, colour and sound for each kind of line (step, command, done, warning, problem, hint)
- A colour strip showing what each run was made of
- Filters (All, Problems, Warnings, Steps), search inside a log, line numbers
- Commands, flags, paths, links, versions and brackets coloured so they read at a glance

### Look and feel
- Dark and light themes, many colours and no pink, readable contrast everywhere
- Vector animations and sounds for every action; Off, Subtle and Lively motion; sound packs
- Fits small windows: nothing overlaps or scrolls sideways down to 420 × 260

### Safety and upkeep
- Cup checks for a newer Cup when it starts, and updates itself with Pour
- Problems page in Settings: errors and crashes are kept on your computer, can be copied or cleared, and are sent anonymously only if the daily hello is on
- Signed and notarised on macOS; installers for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows, Linux and FreeBSD

## Paid

None. Cup is free.
